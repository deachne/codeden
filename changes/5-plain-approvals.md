- **Approvals in plain words.** Turn on **Explain approvals in plain words** under Settings → Utility model (off by default), and every approval card says what the action does, why the agent needs it, whether it can be undone, how risky it is (green, yellow or red), and whether to approve. The command moves under **Details**, and the inbox shows the plain line instead of the command. CodeDen decides whether it can be undone and how risky it is from the action itself, never from what the agent says. If the model doesn't answer within 15 seconds, the card looks as it always has.
- **Delete to the Trash.** A per-project switch in Settings → General tells that project's agents to move files to the Trash instead of deleting them for good.
