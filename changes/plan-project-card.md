- **Plan this project?** Adding a project now puts a card in your Inbox, however you added it (the projects page, the orchestrator, or restoring an archived one). It has **Plan with me** and **Open project**, says so once a plan is ready for you to check, and closes itself when you approve the plan or archive the project. Dismissing all notices leaves it, since it asks something of you.
