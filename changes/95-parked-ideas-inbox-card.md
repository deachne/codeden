- **A green "New ideas parked by the scout" card in the Inbox.** When the scout parks ideas, one card per project lists up to 5 of them in plain words ("and N more" after that), with **Open parked ideas** and **Dismiss**. A later round updates the open card instead of adding another. Dismissing only closes the card; the ideas stay in the list. It shows under All only and isn't counted as waiting on you.
