- **Attach a picture to an answer.** On a question card an agent asks you, you can now attach a photo or screenshot (up to 4, each up to 5 MB) with an "Attach a picture" button or by pasting. You see a small preview first and can remove it. The agent gets the picture with your answer. A file that isn't a picture, or one that's too big, is refused with a plain message, and an assistant that can't look at pictures says so on the card.
