- **A live score for each builder model on the Usage page.** A new table, "How each builder model does on your real work", tallies the factory's own reviews across your projects (or the one project the page is filtered to) for the last 7, 30 or 90 days: changes finished, how often they passed review first time, review rounds per change, merges reverted, cost per merged change and what is still in progress. A model with fewer than five finished changes shows its count and "Too few to judge" instead of percentages, and work CodeDen has no price for reads "Not priced", never $0.00. It is worked out from what the factory already records, so nothing new is collected.
