- **Recipes: step-by-step jobs that pause for your OK.** In Automations, "What it does" now offers **A recipe (several steps)**: a list CodeDen runs in order, written in plain words. Each step starts an agent session, tells the PM, or **pauses and asks you** a question (for example "OK to go on?"). Yes carries on to the next step, No stops the recipe there. The run view shows which step it is on and what it is waiting for, with Yes and No buttons, and the same question appears as a card in your inbox. A recipe keeps its place if CodeDen restarts: it picks up at the step that wasn't finished and never repeats one that was. Like every automation, a recipe made or changed by an agent starts switched off, its sessions never get more permissions than you approved, and in a dark factory it stops and flags you instead of answering for you. There is no release step, branching or loops yet.
