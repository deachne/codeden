- **Archive a project from the sidebar.** A project's menu (right-click it, or the **⋯** button) has **Archive project**: after a confirmation the project leaves the list, its automations and factory stop, and the worktrees of its sessions are cleaned up (any with uncommitted changes are kept). Your folder isn't touched, and adding the same folder again brings the project back. Sessions that are still working have to be stopped first.
