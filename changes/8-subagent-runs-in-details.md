- **Subagent runs in Session details.** A new section lists every subagent run in the session with its status, agent type, cost, tokens and time, and adds up their cost and tokens. Click a run to open its thread. Runs in earlier turns that aren't loaded yet are counted with their cost. The figures only break down the session's own spend and never add to its total. Subagent costs are now explained in the usage and costs guide.
