- **Second opinions on a plan.** Next to **Approve plan** there is now an optional **Get second opinions** button. A small box shows which models will review the plan (three by default, from other companies than the Planner where they are ready), a rough cost and a cost limit for each reviewer, and nothing starts until you press **Go**. The reviewers read the plan on their own and read-only, then the Planner checks each point against the real code and gives you a table: the point, who raised it, whether it agrees, its call and why. Rows only you can decide are marked. Accept or reject each row, then **Add accepted to plan** sends the accepted ones back to the Planner to fold into the plan. A reviewer that goes over its limit is stopped and the Planner works with what came back. Approve plan works as before without any of this.
