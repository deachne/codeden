- **Agents you can read at a glance.** Status dots are bigger, one colour per state (needs you, working, done, failed, idle). Only the needs-you dot pulses, slowly, and it stops once you open the agent; Settings > General can turn the pulse off. Agent lists put needs-you agents first, then failed ones. A finished agent's output starts with a green Done or red Failed banner (with a one-line reason) and a two-line plain summary saying whether you need to act.
