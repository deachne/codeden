- **Scout ideas are labelled `scout` in the Ideas repository.** Ideas the factory's scout parks are now copied with a `scout` label beside the project label, so they can be told apart from the owner's own ideas on the Ideas board.
- **Scout ideas carry their own project's label.** A parked idea is labelled with the name of the project its scout was checking (for example `emoji-it` or `sundog-radio`) instead of always `codeden`.
